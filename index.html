ABOUT ME

Building practical digital experiences.

I'm Eunice Ogwilo, a web developer based in Kenya, focused on building responsive and user-friendly websites for businesses, organizations and individuals.

My technical foundation includes HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, PHP and C++. I also work with Git, GitHub, Figma and AI-assisted development tools. I continue to strengthen my skills through hands-on projects and practical development work.

My portfolio includes business, agricultural and personal web projects, as well as branding work. I enjoy solving practical problems through technology and continuously improving my development skills.
